# Trademark policy

The **chitra** name and logo are trademarks of Deveshwar Jaiswal.

The code is [Apache-2.0](LICENSE) and you may do a great deal with it. The name
is separate. Section 6 of that license grants no trademark rights on purpose,
and this document says what is nevertheless fine.

The point is narrow: someone should always be able to tell whether they are
looking at chitra or at something else. Nothing here is meant to make it harder
to use, fork, package, or write about the project.

## You do not need permission to

- **Use chitra**, in any setting, commercial or otherwise.
- **Fork or modify it**, and distribute your version under Apache-2.0.
- **Say what your software does.** Accurate factual statements are fine —
  "built on chitra", "compatible with chitra", "a chitra plugin",
  "imports chitra graphs".
- **Package it** for a distribution, registry, or internal mirror, under the name
  `chitra`, so long as it is a build of this project and you have not made
  changes that a user would be surprised by.
- **Write about it.** Articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons, reviews —
  including critical ones. Use the name and the logo as illustration.
- **Keep the name in your fork's history**, source files, and attribution
  notices. Retaining it is required by the license, not a violation of this
  policy.

## Please ask first before

- **Naming your product or service after it.** Do not release a fork, a hosted
  service, or a commercial offering *called* chitra, or called something
  confusingly close to it. Name it your own thing and describe its lineage in
  prose.
- **Using the name or logo in a way that suggests we made, endorse, or support
  your product.** In particular, do not use it as your company name, app name,
  domain, or social handle.
- **Modifying the logo**, or using it as your own icon.

## Rebranding a fork

If you fork and ship something meaningfully different, give it its own name.

> ✅ "Foobar — a code-graph tool, originally forked from chitra"
> ❌ "chitra Pro" · "chitra Cloud" · "Foobar chitra"

## Asking

Open an issue or email deveshwar.jaiswal996@gmail.com. Permission for reasonable
uses is normally granted, in writing, and quickly.
